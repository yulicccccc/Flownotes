All standard cards default to `width: max-content; max-width: 520px;`, hugging the text tightly with 0px trailing dead space; 3) Zero-Loss Storage Healing (`sanitizeGraph`): Automatically removes legacy `customWidth` and `customHeight` from all `!n._userResized` nodes across stored maps; 4) Full First-Line Title Recovery: Detects and repairs cards whose `label` was cut off by `slice(0, 42)` from `content`, restoring full title readability and eliminating unearned `[📝]` badges; 5) Import Sanitation: Removed `slice(0, 42)` and hardcoded `customWidth` from `importExcalidrawFile`.
